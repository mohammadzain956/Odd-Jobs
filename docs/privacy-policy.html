Odd Jobs - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 July 2026

Odd Jobs is a marketplace where people post small local jobs and workers accept them. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how you can get rid of it. We keep it short because it should be readable.

What we collect

  • Your account: the name you choose and your email address.
  • Your posts: the job title, description, category, pay, city and area, and any photos you upload.
  • Your messages: chat between you and the other person on a job.
  • Your reviews: the rating and comment you leave after a job is finished.
  • Your city, if you ask for it: when you tap "Near me", the app reads your device location once to work out which city you are in. We store the city name, not your coordinates. You can skip this and pick your city from a list.
  • Notification token: an anonymous device identifier so we can send you a push notification when you get a message. It is not linked to your contacts or anything else on your device.

What we do not collect

  • We do not collect your phone number, contacts, photos other than the ones you deliberately attach to a post, or continuous location tracking.
  • We do not sell your data. We do not run advertising trackers.
  • We do not handle payments. Payment for a job happens directly between you and the other person.

Who can see what

  • Public: your job posts, your display name, your rating and reviews.
  • Only you and the other person on a job: your chat messages.
  • Only you: your saved jobs, and your email address.

Automated moderation

New posts are checked automatically before they go live, so that illegal, unsafe, or fraudulent listings do not reach other users. The text of the post is sent to an AI moderation service (Anthropic) for this check. Your name, email, and messages are not sent. Posts that look suspicious are held for a human to review.

Where your data is stored

Data is stored with Supabase, which hosts our database and file storage on secure cloud servers. Access is restricted by database security rules so that users can only read the data this policy says they can.

Deleting your account

In the app: open Profile, scroll to the bottom, and tap Delete my account. You will be asked to confirm.

By email: if you cannot access the app, write to tahirzain06@gmail.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete it. We will action it within 30 days.

Deleting your account permanently removes:

  • your account and email address,
  • your open job posts,
  • your chat messages, saved jobs, reviews, uploaded photos, and notification token.

Jobs you were still working on with someone else are kept for that other person's records, but your name is removed from them and replaced with "Deleted user". This is so that one person deleting their account does not erase the other person's job history.

Children

Odd Jobs is not intended for anyone under 18. Do not create an account if you are under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.
